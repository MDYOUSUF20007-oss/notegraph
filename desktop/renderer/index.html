NoteGraph Board & Graph Field Guide

KNOWLEDGE GRAPH How to Make & Connect Graph Nodes
  • Every Board / Note is a Node: Click + New Board to create a node. Give every note a real title (≥ 2 characters) instead of leaving it as Untitled.
  • Link with [[Wiki-Links]]: Switch to Notes (Ctrl+2) and click [[ Link Note or type [[Target Title]] anywhere inside your note or inside a Sticky Note card on the Infinite Board.
  • Create Missing Targets on Click: Click any unresolved chip in Outgoing Links to auto-create and link that note right away. Mentioning another note's exact title also generates edges & Backlinks automatically.
  • Graph Navigation: Drag any node circle to reorganize clusters, scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan, or click a node to open its board.
CANVAS & LASSO Infinite Board, Lasso Drag Move & Connectors
  • Smooth Lasso Drag & Move: Select the Lasso tool (S) and circle strokes. Drag directly inside the dashed selection box to glide strokes freely in any direction — or click Drag Move in the toolbar to drag from anywhere on the canvas. Use ← ↑ ↓ → arrows for pixel-precise nudges.
  • Card Connectors (Mind Maps): Click Select (V) to add/drag Sticky Notes & Checklists, or press Connect (C) and click two cards to draw a labeled curved arrow between them.
  • PDF Markup & Slides: Import PDFs as multi-slide lecture decks or laid out side-by-side on one canvas, then draw with Pen, Highlighter, Snap shapes, or Rainbow Laser (L).
WORKFLOW & SYNC Shortcuts, Themes & Android .zip Transfer
  • Quick Keys: Ctrl/Cmd+K Quick Switcher · Space+Drag Pan · F11 Classroom Zen Mode · Ctrl/Cmd+V Paste Screenshot onto Board · PageUp/PageDown Next/Prev Slide.
  • Themes & Gradients: Switch between AMOLED, Kindle, Matte/Mac, and Blackboard, plus independent per-theme Gradient presets.
  • Cross-OS Node Sync: Open Sync in the top bar to sync every note, folder, checklist, ink stroke, PDF annotation, graph link & deletion across Windows, macOS & Android via Shared Cloud Folder (iCloud/OneDrive/Dropbox/Drive), Direct Wi-Fi LAN, or Sync v2 .zip.
